# Contributing

Contributions should improve accuracy, clarity, accessibility, and practical usefulness.

## Topic-page structure
- Summary and impact
- How the weakness occurs
- Request/data-flow diagram
- Vulnerable pattern (only when useful and safely framed)
- Recommended secure pattern
- Verification and regression-test ideas
- References to primary sources

## Rules
- Prefer official sources and link directly to relevant guidance.
- State assumptions and framework/version caveats.
- Do not include real credentials, secrets, personal data, or live-target instructions.
- Labs must use systems the learner owns or has explicit permission to test.
- Keep diagrams readable and provide text explanations.
- Do not claim that any checklist or scanner guarantees security.

## Review checklist
- Is the advice technically correct and current?
- Are prevention steps concrete and layered?
- Are examples safe and clearly scoped?
- Do links work and support the claims?
- Can a developer turn the guidance into a test or code-review check?
